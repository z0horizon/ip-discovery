# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, trolling, and other
conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this code.
Report conduct issues privately through the contact path in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as
confidentially as practical. Maintainers who are the subject of a report must
recuse themselves from its handling.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project or its community.

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
